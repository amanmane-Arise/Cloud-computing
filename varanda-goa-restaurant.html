Our story

A kitchen shaped by every ship that ever docked here

Goa's tables were never just one cuisine — Portuguese vinegar and chillies, Konkan coconut and kokum, and later the Chinese and continental dishes that found their way into every beach shack menu. Varanda cooks all of it under one roof, the way a Goan household actually eats through the week.

We use tandoor and coconut both, recheado paste and soy sauce both. Nothing here is a compromise between cuisines — each dish is made properly, in its own tradition, and served on the same table.
